2. Platform Description

MyPlopPlop is a multi-service platform operating in Haiti that provides:

  • Marketplace: An online marketplace connecting customers with local merchants (restaurants, supermarkets, pharmacies, hardware stores, and more)

7. Sol (Rotating Savings Groups)

Sol is a digital version of the traditional Haitian rotating savings group (ROSCA). By participating in a Sol group, you agree to the following:

  • Commitment: When you join a Sol group, you commit to making all required contributions for the full cycle duration
  • Contributions: You must make your contribution on time for each cycle. Late or missed payments may result in removal from the group and account penalties
  • Payouts: Payouts follow the order determined by the group administrator (which may be randomized). You will receive your payout when it is your turn in the rotation
  • Fees: A platform fee of 2% is applied to each contribution. A withdrawal fee of 1.5% applies when transferring Sol payouts to external payment methods. An access fee of 150 HTG applies to join groups
  • Group Size: Sol groups have a minimum of 3 and maximum of 30 members
  • Risk: While we facilitate Sol groups digitally, participation involves inherent financial risk. MyPlopPlop is not a bank and does not guarantee the performance of Sol group members
  • Removal: Group administrators may remove members who fail to meet their contribution obligations
